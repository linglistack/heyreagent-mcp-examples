# ChatGPT setup

ChatGPT connects with your HeyReagent account sign-in. No API key needed.

## Steps

1. Sign in at [heyreagent.com](https://heyreagent.com) and connect LinkedIn on your thread page.
2. In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Security and login, and turn on Developer mode.
3. Go to ChatGPT Plugins, press the plus button, type a name and a description, enter `https://api.heyreagent.com/mcp` as the address, and create the connection.
4. Sign in to HeyReagent when ChatGPT asks.
5. In a chat, choose Developer mode from the plus menu and select it.

Then ask it for what you want in plain words. It sees each HeyReagent action as a tool. Paste any recipe from [recipes/](../recipes/find-decision-makers.md).

## Notes

- Developer mode availability depends on your ChatGPT account type. If you do not see it, check OpenAI's own guide on which accounts have it.
- Screens change. The official walkthrough is at [heyreagent.com/docs](https://heyreagent.com/docs), under "Use it from Claude or ChatGPT".
